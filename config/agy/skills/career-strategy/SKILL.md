# CAREER, RESEARCH & SCHOLARSHIP STRATEGY

## Goal & Positioning
- **Target Path:** Graduate scholarships (MSc/PhD, Industrial PhD, EngD) via 100% dry-lab applied research and high-income technical HealthTech/MedTech roles.
- **Positioning Moat:** Never position me as a generic coder. Target niches where MD clinical expertise is a defensible moat and human clinical accountability resists AI replacement.
- **Scholarship Fallback:** If direct PhD, Industrial PhD, or EngD admission fails (due to prerequisite, language, or sponsor gaps), bridge via a funded 1–2 year applied MSc with an industry internship or thesis.
- **Interview-Ready Learning:** Frame technical explanations, project design choices, and trade-offs so I can clearly defend them in PI and technical job interviews.

## 3 Target Domains (Software, Data, AI & Clinical Validation Only)
1. **Clinical Data, AI & Validation:** Clinical/Health Informatics, Clinical/Health Data Science, Clinical Decision Support Systems (CDSS), Medical AI / Machine Learning for Healthcare, Regulatory Science (Software as a Medical Device / SaMD, AI Validation).
2. **Medical Imaging & Smart Surgery:** Medical Imaging / Computer-Aided Diagnosis, Surgical Data Science, Computer-Assisted Intervention (CAI), Medical Extended Reality (XR/VR/AR), Medical Robotics (Human-Robot Interaction).
3. **Biosignals, Wearables & Rehab/DTx:** Digital Biomarkers, Wearable Sensors / Mobile Health (mHealth), Physiological Signal Processing, Digital Therapeutics (DTx), Rehabilitation/Neuroengineering, Biomechanics, Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI), Healthcare Human-Computer Interaction (HCI).

## Career & Project Exclusions (Do Not Recommend as Target Tracks)
- Pure management or health economics
- Pharmacology / Drug Discovery
- Genomics / Omics / Bioinformatics
- Wet-lab research
- Low-level hardware / circuits
- Pure theoretical math proofs
